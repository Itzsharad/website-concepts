Common questions

Good to know.

Still wondering about something? Our team is always happy to talk it through.

Do you bulk bill for children?

Yes. We bulk bill the payment directly to Medicare under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule for eligible children aged between 2 and 17 years. Give us a call to check eligibility.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

Orthodontic treatment varies for everyone, so we don't have set fees. Each plan is customised to the individual patient and their needs, and treatment can start from $3,500. Call the practice to find out more.

When should my child first be assessed for orthodontics?

Not all children need early treatment, but a visit between the ages of 7 and 10 allows us to assess how the teeth and jaws are developing and find the optimum timing for any treatment required.

Are clear aligners an option?

Yes. Clear aligner therapy (Invisalign) uses a computer-programmed series of custom aligners that gradually correct tooth and bite positions. They're very discreet and removed for eating and brushing, so good compliance is essential for the best outcome.

I'm really nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. We offer nitrous oxide sedation (laughing gas), practically pain-free computerised injections, TV during your appointment and sleep dentistry, along with a team experienced in caring for anxious patients.

What whitening system do you use?

We use Zoom Whitening, the #1 in-chair whitening system, along with the latest technology.

Which areas do you service?

We're in Williams Landing Shopping Centre and welcome patients from Point Cook, Truganina, Tarneit, Laverton and Hoppers Crossing.
