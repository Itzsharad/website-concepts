What is IDD Therapy?

Precision decompression, exactly where it hurts.

Intervertebral Differential Dynamics (IDD) Therapy is a trusted non-surgical spinal decompression treatment. Many back pain conditions sit at a particular level of the spine, so the SPINA medical device gently draws apart that targeted segment, relieving pressure on specific discs and nerves while stretching tight muscles and stiff ligaments.

It's a step change from traditional traction. Combined with manual therapy, we aim to relieve pain and create a platform for long-term functional improvement.

Does IDD Therapy hurt?

No. IDD Therapy is intended to be completely comfortable. Considerable forces are used to decompress the spine, but they're applied in a controlled manner. We play gentle music and most people fall asleep. We're very used to the sound of snoring.

How many treatments will I need?

The programme is based around a 20-treatment protocol, as it takes time to change something that often built up over years. Most suitable patients have an initial course of 10 sessions, followed by a further block of 10.

How long is each treatment?

About one hour: ten minutes of FAR infrared heat to warm the muscles and ligaments, 25 minutes on the SPINA machine, then cold therapy to calm the treated area.

I've had surgery. Is IDD Therapy still suitable?

Provided there is no surgical hardware (metalwork etc.) and your surgery was over six months ago, IDD Therapy can be provided.

Do I need an MRI scan?

We use MRI scans to confirm the level of the spine to treat and rule out anything that would make IDD Therapy inappropriate. If you don't have one, we can still see you, and arrange a scan if IDD Therapy is deemed appropriate (self-pay: £200 at weekends, £250 on weekdays).

What if I want to stop treatment?

You're under no obligation to continue. If you've paid for a programme, we'll refund the balance of any treatments not taken.
