Our story

A Montclair name for New York moves.

Gibraltar Van Lines works out of 8 Gates Ave in Montclair, New Jersey, just across the river from the city. It's a name built on an idea as solid as the rock it's named for: when you hand over everything you own, you should feel completely steady about it.

That means a real conversation before anything gets packed, a clear plan for the day, and people who treat your home with the same respect you do.
