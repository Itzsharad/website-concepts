02About us

A moving company from the heart of Manhattan, for people who want the whole house handled with care.

Full House Movers NYC works out of East 24th Street, a short walk from Gramercy and Kips Bay. That means we know what moving in this city really involves: narrow stairwells, tight elevators, busy streets and buildings with their own rules.

Our name says it plainly. Whether it's a single room or every last thing you own, we treat the job as one complete move, not a pile of boxes.
