FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Knowledge is best shared. If you don't see your question here, give us a call.

Where does Intertech work?

We're headquartered in Austin, Texas, with an office in San Antonio, and we serve commercial clients throughout Texas and the Southwest.

What types of flooring do you install?

Carpet & resilient, tile & stone, wood flooring, access flooring, sports flooring, coatings & finishes, and polished concrete.

How long have you been in business?

Intertech was founded in Austin in 1988, so we bring over 30 years of commercial flooring experience to every project.

Can you help with more than installation?

Yes. Our team supports your project from budgeting to project management to beautifully installed floors and everything in between.

Our data center is adding equipment. Should we assess our raised floor?

Servers have grown much heavier in recent years. Assessing your raised access floor before new equipment arrives helps avoid emergency repairs and unplanned downtime, and reinforcement options are often simpler and more affordable than people expect.

What does Starnet membership mean for my project?

It gives us national buying power, an unmatched product selection and consistent quality standards, combined with the personal service of a locally owned business.
