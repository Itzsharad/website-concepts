Are you suffering…

01

Back Pain

The spine protects nerves running from brain to tailbone, so back problems can refer pain almost anywhere — shoulder pain, frozen shoulder, elbow pain and tennis elbow are commonly linked.

02

Sciatica

Shooting, stabbing or burning pain from the low back, through the buttocks and down the legs. An MRI diagnosis of a disc bulge isn't the end of the story — there are real solutions.

03

Neck Pain

Poor alignment and forward head posture overload the neck, referring pain up into headaches or down into the shoulders, elbows and arms. A stiff neck often starts lower down your spine.

How is ABC™ different from regular chiropractic?

Regular chiropractic typically adjusts individual joints to relieve symptoms. Advanced Biostructural Correction™ is a whole-body method that corrects the spinal misalignments your body can't fix on its own, restoring upright, balanced posture.

What happens at my initial assessment?

You'll complete our new patient forms, then receive a full examination including your first treatment and results — currently £49.

Can poor posture really cause rib pain and chest tightness?

Yes. When your body falls forward the rib cage compresses, the joints where your ribs meet your spine stop moving properly, and the muscles between the ribs tighten to hold you up.

Is ECC™ the same as Cranial Facial Release?

No. CFR is a balloon-assisted cranial technique developed in the USA and rarely available in the UK. We use a related but distinct method: ABC Endonasal Cranial Correction™.

I've had an MRI showing a disc bulge. Can you still help?

An MRI diagnosis isn't the end of the road. Get in touch and we'll assess whether structural correction is right for your sciatica.

Does treatment involve drugs, surgery or endless exercises?

No. Our approach is natural — without surgery, injections or reliance on prescription drugs, and without hours of endless exercises.
