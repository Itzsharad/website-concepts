Meet the doctor

Experience you can feel.

Dr. Christine Hughes brings a multidisciplinary background, specialised training in spinal trauma and a deep belief in whole-body wellness to every visit.

Before relocating to Austin, Dr. Hughes was part of a multidisciplinary practice at St. Joseph Medical Plaza in downtown Houston, an approach to collaborative care that still shapes the way Airport Chiropractic works today.

She is passionate about educating patients on health, wellness and preventing disease, so you leave not only feeling better, but understanding why. And for Spanish-speaking patients: se habla español.
