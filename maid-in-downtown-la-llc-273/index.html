Our story

Local to Downtown LA, personal by nature.

Maid In Downtown La LLC is a house cleaning service based right on S Grand Ave, in the middle of the city we clean for. The name says it all: this is a business made here, for the people who live here.

City life moves fast. Between work, traffic and everything else LA asks of you, keeping a home spotless can slip down the list. That's where we come in, taking care of the cleaning so your home stays a place to breathe, not another job waiting for you.

When you call, you're talking to a local team, not a faceless booking platform. Tell us about your space and how you live in it, and we'll take it from there.
