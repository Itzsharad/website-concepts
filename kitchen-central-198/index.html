(04) — The Process

Seven steps from first call to final walk-through.

A clear, guided path, with a field manager keeping you updated and an online portal so you can follow along.

01

Book a designer

You call us to make an appointment for a designer to visit your home.

02

Measure & listen

We take measurements, listen to your vision, budget and timeline, and show you our product options.

03

The magic begins

We design your new kitchen, visualised with our design software so you can request any adjustments.

04

Showroom presentation

We present your designs and the full range of options: colour, samples, door style, crown moulding. We finalise the plan and set an installation date.

05

Final confirmation

Our field manager visits with the designer to confirm measurements and the final plan.

06

Work begins

We protect floors, tape off walls and safely remove appliances. Updates come from your field manager and our online portal.

07

Final walk-through

Your field manager returns to ensure you're fully satisfied with your beautiful kitchen or bathroom.
