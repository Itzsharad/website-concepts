02Our storyEst. 1997
Greenvale, VIC

Regain your smile, without the stress.

Excellent care, modern equipment and diligent customer service, since we opened our doors in 1997.

Greenvale Dentistry began as a solo practice led by Dr Alfonso Sammassimo. In 2010 the practice relocated to its current home, and the team has since grown into a vibrant multi-practitioner clinic.

Today our multi-purpose facility comprises four modern dental clinics, and will soon include an operating theatre and recovery room, so that modern dental procedures can be accommodated under one roof.

Dr Alfonso Sammassimo

BDSc (Melb) · Cert Masters Implant Dentistry (gIDE)

Dr Sammassimo graduated from the University of Melbourne in 1996 and immediately commenced private practice. He has since mentored and worked alongside a team of high-quality dentists and hygienists.

With a special interest in dental implantology, he completed a mini residency at the renowned Malo Clinic in Lisbon and a Masters program in Implantology with the gIDE Institute in Los Angeles, and is part of the gIDE alumni group under the mentorship of Dr Sascha Jovanovic.

05Leading-edge technology

Precision you can't see, comfort you can feel.

Our patients expect a premium level of care and technical support, which is why we invest in the latest technology to plan, coordinate and operate with efficiency and precision.

01

Digital radiology

X-ray images viewed digitally in our intraoral radiology suites generally require a third of the radiation dose of conventional X-ray film.

Magnification that aids our dentists during emergency procedures, when finesse and attention to detail matter most.

Digital intraoral cameras provide optimal visibility before and during your procedure.

08Questions

Good to know.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, new patients are always welcome. For your first appointment, please complete a medical history form and bring it with you. Our team can help with the form when you call.

When are you open?

We're open six days a week. Monday to Friday we offer extended trading hours, including early-morning and evening appointments. Saturdays are 8.30am – 2.00pm with limited availability, and we're closed on Sundays.

Do you see children?

Absolutely. We encourage children to visit our clinic to promote healthy habits early on, and several of our dentists have a particular interest in family dentistry.

Can you help with a dental emergency?

Yes. We act as an emergency dentist for Melbourne residents, treating urgent teeth damage and reviewing long-term treatment options when required. Call us on (03) 9333 6403.

Do you offer Invisalign®?

Yes. Dr Huong Nguyen is an accredited Invisalign® provider, and we also offer modern orthodontic teeth straightening techniques at our clinic.

What are mini dental implants?

Mini dental implants can replace a bridge of teeth with only four implants, or be used to stabilise dentures. Dr Alfonso Sammassimo has a special interest in implantology and completed a Masters program in Implantology with the gIDE Institute.

Are your dentists qualified?

All of the general and cosmetic dentists and dental hygienists at our Greenvale clinic are members of the Australian Dental Association.

Time for a check-up?

Your smile deserves this kind of care.

Our friendly staff are keen to assist you. Contact us today to schedule an appointment or consultation.
