Treatments

Holistic care for pain relief, posture & mobility.

A wide toolkit of chiropractic techniques — chosen and combined for your body, your condition and your lifestyle. Natural, effective, drug-free.

Core care

Chiropractic Spinal & Extremity Adjustments

Precise, non-invasive adjustments by hand or instrument to correct alignment, reduce tension and help your body heal itself.

Soft tissue

Active Release Therapy

Targeted pressure, stretches and movement to break down adhesions and scar tissue — ideal for stubborn, chronic problems.

Pregnancy

Webster Technique

A specialised technique for mums-to-be that releases two ligaments, two bones and two muscles — giving baby room to settle into place.

Babies, children & jaw

Craniosacral & TMJ Therapy

Gentle corrections for little ones with colic, reflux, flat head or feeding issues — and relief for jaw-related headaches and neck pain.

Muscle pain

Trigger Point & Myofascial Therapy

Specific finger pressure "deactivates" painful knots and releases fascial tightness to improve circulation and muscle healing.

Tendons

Shockwave Therapy

A non-surgical treatment for chronic tendon and muscle pain that boosts your body's own regenerative capabilities.

SOT Therapy & Denneroll Wedges

Sacro Occipital Technique to balance structure and stabilise the pelvis, with Denneroll wedges for a healthy neck curve.

Traction for Neck & Low Back

Manual or mechanical decompression to ease muscle spasm and nerve irritation.

Sports Taping

Supportive taping for ankles and hands to protect ligaments and lower the chance of re-injury.

Nutrition, Diet & Exercises

Guidance that protects the good work of every adjustment and supports musculoskeletal health.

TENS Electrotherapy

Gentle electrical stimulation to block pain signals and improve range of motion.

Ultrasound Therapy

Warms the injured area to boost local blood flow, reduce swelling and break down scar tissue.

How much does a chiropractor visit cost?

Pricing depends on the type of visit and whether it's your first consultation or a follow-up adjustment. Consultation fees start from $100, while treatment fees start from $90.

What happens at my first visit?

Your chiropractor carries out a thorough assessment to understand your condition, then recommends a personalised care plan and explains the treatment before you begin.

Who do you treat?

Families of every age — newborns, children, students, adults, athletes and seniors. Each person receives a care plan tailored to their age, condition and lifestyle.

Do you offer care during pregnancy?

Yes. We offer pregnancy and postnatal care, including the Webster Technique — a specialised chiropractic technique for pregnant patients.

Is chiropractic care drug-free?

Yes. Our approach combines manual adjustments, tailored Active Release Therapy and corrective exercises for natural, drug-free relief.
