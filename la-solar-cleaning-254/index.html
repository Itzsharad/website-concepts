FAQ

Good questions.

Why should I clean my solar panels?

Dirt on the surface of a panel reduces how much light that area can absorb, which reduces its efficiency. Regular cleaning helps your panels keep delivering the savings you bought them for.

What do you clean the panels with?

We gently scrub with a highly concentrated, eco-friendly cleaning solution, combining biodegradable cleansers with safe pressure to remove mildew, algae and dirt.

Why not just call a window washer?

Our technicians are experienced solar pros, trained to identify issues with your system while they clean, not just wipe the glass.

What other services do you offer?

Alongside solar panel cleaning, we offer window cleaning, pressure washing, bird proofing and gutter cleaning.
