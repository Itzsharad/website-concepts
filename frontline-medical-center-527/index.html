Good to know

Still have a question? Our front desk is happy to help on 04 397 9702.

Where is the clinic, and is there parking?

We're in the heart of Al Karama at 106, B-Block, Al Attar Centre, above Chappan Bhog and opposite Spinneys on Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed St. Ample RTA parking is available at the front and back of the premises.

Can I reach you by metro or bus?

Yes. We're between ADCB (Karama) and Burjuman metro stations, less than a 5-minute walk from ADCB. There's also a bus stop in front of the clinic served by buses 33, 44, C5, 81, 21, 25, 91 and C10.

Do you accept insurance?

All major insurance providers are affiliated with us, so your visit can be as hassle-free as possible.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. Our team cares for patients of every age, from children to older adults, and we offer Pedodontics (children's dentistry).

Is root canal treatment painful?

Our experts use the latest technology and techniques for a painless root canal experience, including rotary endodontics, apex locators for precision, 3D obturation, laser-assisted disinfection and bio-ceramic sealers for the best healing.

How long is recovery after an extraction?

We typically advise resting for about 48–72 hours so the area can clot, after which most people return to normal activity. The soft tissue usually heals fully in about 3–4 weeks.
