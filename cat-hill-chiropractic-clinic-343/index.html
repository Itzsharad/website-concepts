Our story

A family practice at the bottom of Cat Hill.

Patients travel to us from all over north London and Hertfordshire, and many have become part of the furniture. That is exactly how we like it.

Cat Hill Clinic was established as a chiropractic clinic in 1997 and has since grown to incorporate therapeutic massage and physiotherapy, giving our patients a genuinely integrated approach to their care.

We strive for high standards of professional, ethical care in a relaxed and friendly environment, welcoming people of all ages and abilities with a wide variety of conditions.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Our reception team is always happy to help on 020 8275 0656.

Who do you treat?
Everyone. We are a family practice welcoming people of all ages and abilities, with patients coming to us from all over north London and Hertfordshire.
What problems can you help with?
We see a wide variety of conditions, including back and neck pain, leg pain, headaches and sports injuries, to name but a few. Not sure? Give reception a call.
Is there parking at the clinic?
Parking is easy on Belmont Avenue or the surrounding streets, and there are no parking restrictions.
Are you open on Saturdays?
Yes, on alternate Saturdays from 9am to 12.30pm. Hours do vary, so please contact reception for current times.
How do I book physiotherapy?
Please contact James Galea directly on 07782 171297 or email james@elementsphysiotherapy.com.
What is the nearest Tube station?
Cockfosters is closest, around a 10 to 15 minute walk. From Oakwood, the 307 bus towards Barnet takes about 5 minutes and stops opposite the clinic.

How to find us

  • From the M25, junction 24: drive towards Cockfosters, past the tube station and shops.
  • At the Cat Hill roundabout (by the Chicken Shed Theatre), take the 3rd exit down Cat Hill.
  • We are about halfway down, on the corner of Belmont Avenue.
  • Parking: easy on Belmont Avenue or surrounding streets, with no parking restrictions.
  • From Enfield: 307 bus towards Barnet, approximately 20 minutes. It stops opposite the clinic on Cat Hill.
  • From Barnet: 307 bus towards Enfield, approximately 10 minutes. It stops halfway up Cat Hill just past the clinic; we are just 30 yards back down the hill.
  • Oakwood: take the 307 bus towards Barnet (5 minutes) and get off at the stop opposite the clinic.
  • Cockfosters: closer, around a 10 to 15 minute walk. Pass the parade of shops, turn right down Mount Pleasant, take the first left then immediate right down Belmont Avenue. We are at the very bottom on the right, at the junction with Cat Hill.
  • New Barnet: take the 307 bus towards Enfield (10 minutes).
