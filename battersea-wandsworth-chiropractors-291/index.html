Is the first scan really free?

Yes. We offer a free posture and spine scan so you can discover what's really going on before deciding on care.

Do you see babies and pregnant mums?

We do. Chiropractic is for babies and adults of all ages, and we offer dedicated chiropractic for pregnancy and chiropractic for babies.

How do I book?

Book online, call us on 020 7228 7822, or message us on WhatsApp. We typically reply within a day on weekdays.

How do I get to the clinic?

We're at 207 St John's Hill, SW11 1TH. Clapham Junction overground is a 5–10 minute walk, the closest bus stop is Marcilly Road, and there is on-street parking outside.

Which areas do you serve?

We welcome new patients based in South West London, Battersea, Wandsworth and Clapham.
