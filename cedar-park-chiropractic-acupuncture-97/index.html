Meet your doctor

Dr. Jeffrey L. SwansonD.C., CSCS, FASA

Born and raised in Sioux City, Iowa, Dr. Swanson found his calling in 7th grade, when his uncle, Dr. Cliff Meylor, treated him for a sports injury. After seeing the remarkable results, he knew chiropractic would be his life's work.

He studied at Iowa State University, then earned his Bachelor of Science and Doctor of Chiropractic degrees from Palmer College of Chiropractic in Davenport, Iowa, the first chiropractic college in the world. Today he lives in Cedar Park with his wife, Darla, and their children, Carly and Bennett. His brothers, Dr. Jon Swanson and Dr. Justin Swanson, also practice in the Austin area.

“Competent, courteous, convenient and cost-effective care that gets results.”

Questions

Good to know.

Can I be seen the same day?

Same-day appointments with Dr. Swanson are available whenever possible. Give us a call at (512) 335-0641 and we'll find the soonest time. Follow-up visits can be scheduled weeks or months in advance.

Do I have to have needles for acupuncture?

No. We offer needle-less acupuncture as well as traditional needle acupuncture and electrical acupuncture. Dr. Swanson is certified in acupuncture and a Fellow of the Acupuncture Society of America.

Should I see a chiropractor after a car accident?

Our Auto Accident Care offers gentle, drug-free and non-invasive treatment for whiplash and other injuries, helping relieve pain, reduce swelling and inflammation, and restore mobility. Dr. Swanson holds a Whiplash Masters' Certification.

Do you treat children and expecting mothers?

Yes. Our chiropractic pediatrics care supports patients from infancy through adolescence, and our gentle pregnancy care helps relieve discomfort and support proper spinal alignment.

What is SoftWave therapy?

SoftWave is a non-invasive treatment that reduces pain, stimulates healing and improves mobility, helping your body recover naturally. Jaide, our chiropractic assistant, specializes in SoftWave sessions.

Where are you located?

We're at 345 Cypress Creek Rd #103 in Cedar Park, near the Hwy 183 and Cypress Creek intersection, just minutes from Lakeline Mall and easily reached from Round Rock, Bee Cave, Leander, Georgetown, Lago Vista, Lakeway and Austin.
