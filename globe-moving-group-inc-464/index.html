About Globe Moving Group

A move isn't furniture on a truck. It's your home, your work, your next chapter, and it deserves to be handled that way.

Globe Moving Group Inc. is a moving company based on Glendale Avenue in San Diego. We believe the best moves feel calm: clear expectations up front, real people on the phone, and a crew that treats your belongings like they matter.

From the first call to the last piece set down at your new place, our goal is simple: get you from Point A to Point B with as little stress as possible, so the only thing left to do is make it home.
