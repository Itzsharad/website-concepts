Do you treat the whole family?

Yes. Northwest Dental Associates is a Cedar Park family dentist, offering routine checkups and general dental care for patients of all ages, alongside cosmetic dentistry.

Can you help me change how my smile looks?

Absolutely. Dr. Pham offers cosmetic procedures, including smile makeovers, using the latest techniques. We'll talk through your goals and options together before any work begins.

I get anxious at the dentist. What can I expect?

Many patients feel the same. Our goal is an office with an atmosphere of comfort and care, and our team is committed to helping you feel at ease throughout your visit.

Who will be treating me?

Dr. Toai Pham, DDS, who earned his BA at The University of Texas at Austin and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at UT Health Science Center in San Antonio. He has practiced dentistry in Cedar Park for over 26 years.
