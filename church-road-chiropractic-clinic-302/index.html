Our story

Meet Dr. Gopal Handa.

Gopal grew up surrounded by the transformative power of chiropractic care. He watched it help his mother through post-pregnancy issues and his father through chronic shoulder pain, and spent countless hours at his elder sister's clinic, captivated by what he saw.

He qualified at the Anglo European College of Chiropractic (AECC) in Bournemouth. As an intern there, he treated a patient who had endured constant headaches for five years. After her very first adjustment she felt instant relief, and over her care plan she no longer needed the medication she'd relied on for half a decade.

"Over the past 15 years, I have been fortunate to locum and work in over 50 different clinics across the country and abroad. Each experience has reinforced my commitment to providing exceptional wellness care to the community of Bromley."~ Dr. Gopal Handa

Today, integrity, compassion and professionalism guide everything at the clinic, alongside a dedication to ongoing education and research. And he practises what he preaches: regular adjustments, mindful nutrition, meditation, the gym and running.

Why choose our clinic?

Five keys to your optimum health.

We treat patients of all ages and from all walks of life, from newborns to professional athletes. To help you return to your optimum level of health, our care focuses on five key points.

i.

Specific adjustments

Using the Diversified, Gonstead and Activator chiropractic techniques as well as kinesiology to increase spinal movement, reduce nerve interference and build-up of scar tissue.

ii.

Muscle balancing

Specific stretches and exercises to re-establish strength and stamina in supporting muscles.

iii.

Specific spinal rehabilitation

Exercises to strengthen supporting spinal muscles, reducing the possibility of a relapse and limiting the amount of scar tissue formed.

iv.

Cranio-sacral work

A technique to ensure the efficient flow of spinal fluid within the spinal cord, helping restore nerve function to its full potential.

v.

Nutritional & dietary advice

Given where needed, so you can pay attention to and take further care of your own health and wellbeing.
