Insurance coverage available to you

Covered, clearly.

We work hard so accidents don't happen. But if one does, you should know your options. Protection is included in our rates, and additional coverage is available upon request.

Included

Basic Valuation

60¢ per pound, per article

Ensures recovery at 60 cents multiplied by the weight of the item. This coverage is included in the hourly rate.

Additional cost

Actual Cash Value

Fair market value

Ensures recovery at the actual cash value of your lost or damaged item(s), up to the total value you declare. Please speak with your representative for more information.

Additional cost

Full Value

Replacement value

Insures recovery at the full replacement value of your lost or damaged item(s), up to the total value you declare. Deductibles may be offered in combination. Speak with your representative for details.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Richard and Mary are a phone call away.

What's included in the $125/hour rate?

A 26-foot box truck, 2 professional movers, tools to disassemble furniture, and furniture pads and shrink wrap upon request. Basic Valuation Coverage ($.60 per pound per article) is included too.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes, 10% off for active/retired military and senior citizens. Discounts cannot be combined at the time of invoice.

Are you licensed and insured?

We are fully licensed and insured. Additional coverage, Actual Cash Value or Full Value protection, is available at an additional cost.

Will you protect my floors?

Absolutely. We're experienced in moving furniture across all flooring, hardwood or designer laminate, and take all appropriate steps to keep your floors beautiful and damage free.

Can you handle awkward pieces and tricky buildings?

Richard has spent 20+ years learning advanced techniques, including hoisting couches into difficult buildings. Tell us about it during your estimate.
