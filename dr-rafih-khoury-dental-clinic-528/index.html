Gum Diseases & Treatments

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss, and often painless until it’s advanced. We start with a conservative plan: professional cleaning, deep scaling and root planing, and advanced options when needed.

  • Deep scaling
  • Laser therapy
  • Tissue regeneration
  • Soft tissue grafts
  • Pocket elimination surgery

Invisible Orthodontics

Thin, removable clear aligners and lingual braces hidden behind the teeth: straighten your smile without sacrificing comfort or confidence. Easier to clean, gentler on enamel, and fewer hours in the chair.

  • Clear aligners
  • Lingual orthodontics
