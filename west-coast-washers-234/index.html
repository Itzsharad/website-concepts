Before you book.

How quickly will I get my quote?

Estimates are free and come with no obligation. Reach out with your project, and we’ll provide your tailored quote within 24 hours.

Are you insured?

Yes. West Coast Washers is an insured, locally owned and operated company serving the greater San Gabriel Valley.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve homes and businesses throughout Pasadena, South Pasadena, San Marino, Alhambra and San Gabriel.

Will there be any hidden fees?

No. We believe in honest communication and fair pricing: what we quote is what you pay, with no hidden fees or unexpected charges.

Is pressure washing safe for my surfaces?

Our technicians adjust pressure and technique to the specific surface being cleaned, removing dirt and stains without causing damage. For more delicate surfaces, we offer soft washing.

Are your cleaning products eco-friendly?

Yes. We use eco-friendly cleaning solutions and water conservation techniques that clean your property effectively while minimizing environmental impact.

Do you clean commercial properties?

Absolutely. We work with residential homes and a wide range of commercial properties, from storefronts and offices to parking lots.
