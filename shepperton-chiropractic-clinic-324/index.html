Our story

Fresh, dynamic & patient-centred care for the whole family.

For 17 years, Shepperton Chiropractic Clinic has been delivering excellent chiropractic health care to the local community, and the list of happy patients now runs into the thousands.

Led by Dr. Mika (D.C.), a long-standing advocate of the patient's role in their own recovery, our team brings together two fully registered chiropractors and two qualified massage therapists, so you're supported from diagnosis and adjustment right through to recovery.

We're registered with most health insurance companies, making premium care simpler to access.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can't see your question? Call us on 01932 429584, we're happy to help.

Do you treat without drugs or surgery?

Yes. We diagnose the root cause of your problem and fix it without the need for drugs or surgery.

What conditions do you help with?

We specialise in back pain, neck pain, headaches, sports injury and performance, pregnancy and any other bodily aches and pains. We also offer Optimal Performance Golf and TOG Gait Scanner assessments.

Do you accept health insurance?

We are registered with most health insurance companies. Give us a call to check your provider.

Can a chiropractor visit me at home?

Home visits are available on special request in some cases. We also have a chiropractor on call throughout the weekend in case of an emergency.

Is there parking at the clinic?

Yes, ample free parking is available on site. Bus stops are just outside the clinic and Shepperton Rail station is only a 10-minute walk away.

Who is on the team?

The clinic offers the services of two fully registered chiropractors and two qualified massage therapists, led by Dr. Mika (D.C.).
